      Questions to ask a lawyer
        Not legal advice. This demo does not use an AI model, read your files, decide who is at fault, calculate damages, or predict whether a claim would succeed. A licensed lawyer must review applicable law, deadlines, evidence, and fees for your situation.

        Money: what is at stake and what it may cost

        Compensation and legal costs both vary widely. These notes are general. A lawyer can explain how they apply to you.

        What losses do people usually record?
        • Medical and rehabilitation costs.
        • Income lost while you could not work.
        • Repair or replacement of damaged property.
        • Pain, suffering, and loss of enjoyment of life, which are assessed by a court and cannot be added up from receipts.
        Why a loss total is not what you would receive

        A court decides which losses are legally recoverable, and may reduce an award if more than one person shares responsibility. Settlements can differ from both. This is why CasePath shows no estimate of compensation.

        How lawyers commonly charge

        Some lawyers bill by the hour. Others take a percentage of any recovery (a contingency fee) and may charge nothing if there is no recovery. Disbursements, such as expert reports and filing fees, are often charged separately. Ask for the arrangement in writing.

        Are there alternatives to going to court?

        Negotiation, mediation, and formal complaint processes can resolve some disputes at lower cost. A lawyer can say whether one fits your situation.

        Privacy and limits

        What CasePath is not

        CasePath is a concept prototype. It does not give legal advice, form a lawyer–client relationship, decide whether a tort has occurred, or connect you to a lawyer. The assistant is an AI, not a lawyer.

        Deadlines

        Legal claims have time limits that differ by province and type of claim, and some are short. If you are unsure, speak to a licensed lawyer or your provincial law society’s referral service soon.

        How your information is handled

        The form and timeline stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere. If the chat assistant is enabled, the text you type is sent to a server and then to a third-party AI provider to produce a reply. Do not enter names, addresses, account numbers, or medical records.

        Before real use

        A live service needs secure storage, consent and retention policies, access controls, abuse protection, and review by qualified legal counsel.
